# ShopSense Order Policies

## Shipping & Delivery

- Standard delivery: 5–7 business days
- Express delivery: 2–3 business days (additional ₹99 charge)
- Same-day delivery: Available in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad) for orders placed before 12 PM
- Free shipping on orders above ₹499
- Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of confirmation (excluding Sundays and public holidays)

## Order Tracking

- A tracking link is sent via SMS and email once the order is dispatched
- Tracking is available on the ShopSense website under "My Orders"
- Customers can also track orders by entering their Order ID on the tracking page
- Tracking updates may take up to 2 hours to reflect after dispatch

## Order Cancellation

- Orders can be cancelled within 2 hours of placement at no charge
- Orders already dispatched cannot be cancelled — customers must wait for delivery and then raise a return request
- Cancellation requests can be raised via the app, website, or by contacting support

## Order Modifications

- Changes to delivery address, phone number, or product variant are allowed within 1 hour of order placement
- Modifications are not allowed once the order moves to "Packed" status

## Failed Deliveries

- If delivery fails due to customer unavailability, the courier will attempt delivery twice more
- After 3 failed attempts, the package is returned to the warehouse
- A refund is initiated within 5–7 business days after the return is received

## Damaged or Wrong Items

- Report damaged or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery via the app or support chat
- Attach photos of the item and packaging when raising the complaint
- A replacement is dispatched within 2 business days, or a full refund is issued if the item is out of stock
