# ShopSense Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return Eligibility

- Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags intact
- The following categories are NOT eligible for return:
  - Perishable goods (food, flowers, plants)
  - Innerwear, swimwear, and personal hygiene products
  - Digital downloads and software
  - Customised or personalised products

## How to Raise a Return Request

1. Go to "My Orders" in the ShopSense app or website
2. Select the order and click "Return / Exchange"
3. Choose the reason for return and upload photos if applicable
4. Schedule a pickup or drop off at the nearest courier partner location
5. Once the return is received and inspected, refund is processed

## Refund Timelines

- UPI / Net Banking / Wallets: 3–5 business days
- Credit / Debit Card: 5–7 business days
- Cash on Delivery (COD): Refunded to ShopSense wallet within 24 hours, or bank account within 7–10 business days (NEFT transfer)
- ShopSense Wallet refunds are instant

## Exchange Policy

- Exchanges are allowed for size, colour, or variant within 7 days of delivery
- Exchange is subject to availability of the requested variant
- Only one exchange is allowed per order item

## Partial Refunds

- If only part of an order is returned, refund is calculated proportionally
- Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a wrong or damaged item

## Refund Status

- Customers can check refund status under "My Orders → Refund Status"
- Refund confirmation is sent via SMS and email
- If refund is not received within the stated timeline, contact support with Order ID and payment method

## Non-Refundable Scenarios

- Items returned after 7 days without prior approval
- Items that are used, damaged, or missing original packaging
- Orders where return pickup was scheduled but not made available by the customer

## Wrong Size or Colour Received

- If you received a product in the wrong size or colour, report it within 48 hours of delivery
- Go to "My Orders", select the item, and choose "Wrong item received"
- Attach a photo of the product received
- A replacement in the correct size or colour will be dispatched within 2 business days
- If the correct variant is out of stock, a full refund will be issued automatically

## Exchange for Size or Colour

- You can exchange for a different size or colour within 7 days of delivery
- Go to "My Orders" and select "Exchange"
- Exchange is subject to availability of the requested variant
- Only one exchange is allowed per order item
